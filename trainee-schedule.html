The three moments you work with both hands

This is where the job is won: a long cooking time is not dead time, it is a free slot. Each time, a cold task sits on top of a red block — that is deliberate, not a clash.

10:35 → 10:55 The fries are blanching → you peel the velouté vegetables. Stay at the fryer: you never leave hot oil unattended. Peel on the bench next to it, not across the kitchen.
12:20 → 12:45 The velouté simmers for 30 min → you whisk the mayonnaise. Check the pot every 5 minutes: it should barely tremble, never roll at a full boil.
13:05 → 13:35 The velouté is in the blast chiller → you wash and spin the salad. Both containers go to the cold room together at 13:35 — one trip, and the door stays shut.

Method

step by step, for each preparation

Salad

Cold
  1. Trim: remove the core and any damaged leaves.
  2. Wash in 3 changes of cold water with a splash of white vinegar. Lift the salad out, tip the water away, refill — never drain the tub with the salad still in it, the grit settles straight back on the leaves.
  3. Spin dry in two goes, basket half full: overloaded, it does not dry and it bruises the leaves.
  4. Perforated tray, absorbent paper underneath, wrap. Label: product, date, time. Cold room 0–3 °C.

Fries

Hotthen cold
  1. Wash, peel and rinse the potatoes again.
  2. Cut batons of the same thickness: same size, same cooking. Hold them in cold water.
  3. Drain and dry them before the fryer — water makes the oil boil over.
  4. Blanch at 150 °C, 6 min per batch, basket half full, 3 batches. The fry is cooked through but still pale, not coloured.
  5. Spread on a tray in a single layer, blast chill: +63 °C → +10 °C in under 2 h.
  6. Wrap, label, cold room. The second fry at 180 °C happens at service, not now.

Mayonnaise

Cold
  1. Everything at the same temperature: take the yolks and the oil out 15 minutes beforehand.
  2. Yolks, mustard, salt, pepper. Leave 2 minutes for the salt to dissolve, then start whisking.
  3. Add the oil in a thin stream — slowly at first, faster once it takes. Tighten with vinegar or lemon juice.
  4. Adjust the seasoning, cling film on the surface, label. Use-by 24 h: raw egg, we keep it no longer.

Velouté

Cold prepHot cooking
  1. Peel, wash and cut the vegetables to an even size.
  2. Sweat in butter without colouring, cover with stock, season lightly — it reduces.
  3. Simmer 30 minutes with a lid on, skimming. No rolling boil.
  4. Blend, pass through the chinois, adjust. Cream goes in last.
  5. Blast chill in a shallow tray, uncovered while it is still hot.
  6. Wrap, label, cold room.

Three rules that never move

  • Nothing hot ever goes into the cold room: it warms everything else up.
  • Label everything: product, date, time. An unlabelled container gets thrown away.
  • Never walk away from a fryer that is switched on, not even for a minute.
